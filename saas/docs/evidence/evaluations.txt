--- what this shows ---
These are the numbers workflow 15 actually measured, in n8n execution 718; see
../../../workflows/15-evaluation-harness/tests/results-2026-09-12.json. The
dashboard is not showing invented demo metrics.

Read the shape of the regression rather than just the sign. HOT recall is
unchanged at 1.000: the candidate still finds every genuinely HOT lead. What it
adds is two false positives — it predicts 6 HOT where production predicts 4 —
and those two are what drop precision from 1.000 to 0.667.

A candidate that "surfaces more hot leads" is exactly how this change would be
described by whoever proposed it, and it would be true. The gate rejects it
anyway, because more HOT is not a tracked metric and precision is.

"DECISIONS CITING IT: 7" is why the published rubric is immutable: seven stored
receipts reference version 1.2.0 by foreign key, and the database refuses both an
edit to its definition and a delete of the row.
